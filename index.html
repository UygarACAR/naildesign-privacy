Nail Design AI — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-17

Nail Design AI ("we", "the app") respects your privacy. This policy explains what data the app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Summary

  • We do not sell your data or use it for advertising or cross-app tracking.
  • Your hand and nail photos are sent to our AI provider only to create your designs. They are not stored on our servers.
  • The designs you generate are saved on your device, not on our servers.
  • You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from inside the app.

Information we collect

Account information. When you sign in with Apple or email, we receive your email address (or an Apple private relay address) and a user identifier. These are used only to create and secure your account.

Style profile. The preferences you choose during onboarding (for example aesthetic, favorite colors, nail length, finishes, boldness, occasions, and skin tone) are stored on your device and synced to your account so your experience stays personalized across devices. Skin tone is used only so rendered designs look natural on your hand.

Hand and nail photos. When you generate a design, the photo you take or select is sent through our server to our AI provider (OpenAI), together with the style options you picked, to render nail designs onto your photo. We do not keep the photo on our servers after the request finishes.

Generated designs. Design images are stored on your device only. We sync the details of each design (such as occasion, palette, finish, length, complexity, date, and whether you marked it as a favorite) to your account, but not the images themselves.

Usage allowance. We record how many designs your account generates each month so we can apply the monthly generation allowance.

Purchases. Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to manage subscription status. RevenueCat receives purchase and receipt information linked to your account identifier. We never receive your payment card details.

Usage analytics. We collect anonymous app events (for example, a design started or a screen viewed, the app version, and the platform). These are tied to a random identifier generated on your device, not to your name, email, or account.

Device permissions. Camera and photo library access are used only when you take or choose a photo, or when you save a design to your photo library. Reminders (such as a notice before a free trial ends) are scheduled locally on your device. We do not collect a push-notification token.

How we use information

  • To generate nail designs and personalize them to your preferences.
  • To sync your style profile and design history across your devices.
  • To provide and verify your subscription.
  • To maintain, secure, and improve the app.

Third parties

  • OpenAI processes your photo and selected style options to generate designs. Under OpenAI's API terms, this data is not used to train their models. OpenAI may keep it for a limited period (up to 30 days) for abuse monitoring.
  • Supabase provides authentication and stores your account data.
  • RevenueCat manages subscription status.
  • Apple handles Sign in with Apple and in-app purchase payments.

We do not sell personal data or share it for third-party advertising.

Data retention and deletion

Your account data (style profile, design history, and usage records) is kept until you delete it. You can delete your account at any time in the app via Profile → Delete Account & All Data. This permanently removes your account and its data from our servers, and erases your gallery from the device.

Anonymous analytics events are not linked to your account and are not affected by account deletion. Deleting your account does not cancel an active subscription. Cancel it in your device's Settings → your name → Subscriptions. RevenueCat and Apple may keep purchase records as required for billing and tax purposes. You can contact us to ask for any remaining data to be deleted.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete your personal data by contacting us at the address below. We will respond within a reasonable time.

Children

Nail Design AI is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.
