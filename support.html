Frequently asked questions

How does Nail Design AI work?

Take or choose a photo of your hand in the Create tab, pick an occasion, vibe, palette, and finish, and the AI renders nail art directly onto your own nails. Save the looks you love to your Gallery or share them with your nail tech.

Why does generating take a while?

Each design is created from scratch for your photo, which usually takes 30–60 seconds. A stable internet connection helps. If a generation fails, it doesn't count against your allowance. Just try again.

Do I need an account?

Yes. You sign in with Apple or email so your style profile, design history, and subscription stay with your account across devices.

What does Premium include?

Premium unlocks AI design generation with every style and finish option, plus one-tap regeneration. It includes a monthly generation allowance that resets on the 1st of each month. Monthly and annual plans are available, and new subscribers start with a 7-day free trial.

How do I cancel or manage my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Nail Design AI, or Profile → Manage Subscription in the app. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid being charged.

I bought Premium but it's not showing. What now?

In the app, go to Profile → Restore Purchases (or tap "Restore Purchases" on the paywall). Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you used to subscribe.

What happens to my photos?

Your photo is sent securely to our AI provider only to create your designs, and it isn't stored on our servers. Generated designs are saved on your device.

How do I delete my account and data?

In the app, go to Profile → "Delete Account & All Data." This permanently deletes your account and its data from our servers and erases your gallery from the device. It doesn't cancel an active subscription. Cancel that in Settings first.
